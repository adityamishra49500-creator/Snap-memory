Project Proposal: SnapMemory Internal Rollout

Overview
This document proposes an internal pilot of SnapMemory across the product and engineering teams
for Q4. The goal is to reduce time spent searching for meeting outcomes and reference documents.

Objectives
1. Reduce average time-to-find-a-decision from 12 minutes to under 2 minutes.
2. Keep all indexed data fully local to each employee's device for privacy compliance.
3. Support PDF, PPTX, meeting audio, and screenshots as input sources.

Timeline
The pilot begins November 3 and runs for six weeks, concluding December 12.

Success Criteria
The pilot will be considered successful if at least 70% of pilot users report finding
answers to project questions faster than before, and if zero user data leaves the device
during the pilot period.
